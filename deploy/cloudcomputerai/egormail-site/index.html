Not a shared service.
Your own instance.

Most AI email apps put everyone's mail into one big shared system. EgorMail gives each account its own isolated instance: its own database, its own storage, its own app.

  • Your mail is never mixed with other customers' data
  • Isolated database and storage per instance
  • Built on open-source code you can inspect
  • Delete your account and its data from settings, anytime
